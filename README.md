## About Me

I'm an MCA student at NIT Warangal who learns by shipping — I'd rather build a working RAG pipeline or a full checkout flow than just read about one. My work sits at the intersection of two things I care about: **applied AI** and **full-stack engineering**.

Right now that means building agentic systems that actually retrieve and reason over real documents, not just call an API and hope — and pairing that with the full-stack chops to ship the product around them. I'm the kind of developer who opens a new concept (agents, RAG evaluation, payment gateways) and doesn't stop until there's a repo with working code behind it.

## Featured Projects

### 🤖 [Tarka.AI — Agentic RAG Knowledge Platform](https://github.com/Ankitpatidar01/Tarka-AI)

An AI-powered multi-agent conversational platform combining RAG, tool integration, and document retrieval to answer questions grounded in real knowledge sources — not just the model's memory.

- **What it solves:** getting LLMs to answer reliably from a private knowledge base instead of hallucinating
- **What I built:** a document ingestion + chunking pipeline, sentence-transformer embeddings indexed in FAISS/ChromaDB, and a **Router Agent** that classifies each query and dispatches it to retrieval, tool-calling, or conversational workflows
- **Key tech:** Python, LangChain, LangGraph, FAISS, ChromaDB, Sentence Transformers, Streamlit
- **Why it's interesting:** it's a hands-on exploration of agentic workflow design — routing, memory, and tool use — rather than a single-shot chatbot

---

### 🛍️ [CARA — Production-Ready MERN E-Commerce Platform](https://github.com/Ankitpatidar01/CARA-MERN-ECommerce)

A full-stack e-commerce web application built to learn real-world backend architecture end to end — not just CRUD, but auth, payments, and order flow together.

- **What it solves:** the full commerce loop — browsing, cart, secure checkout, and order management — in one cohesive system
- **What I built:** JWT-based authentication with role-based access, a user-specific cart and order pipeline, and Razorpay payment integration with signature verification
- **Key tech:** Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose, JWT, bcrypt, Razorpay
- **Why it's interesting:** it forced me to design a REST API that handles state correctly across auth, cart, and payment — the parts of e-commerce that are easy to fake and hard to get right

---

### 🎤 [AI Interview Preparation Platform](https://github.com/Ankitpatidar01/AI-Interview-Preparation-Platform)

An AI-powered platform for interview prep, combining resume analysis with personalized interview question generation.

- **What it solves:** generic interview prep — this ties the questions to the candidate's actual resume
- **What I built:** a full-stack app structure (separate frontend/backend) designed around resume parsing feeding into AI-generated, personalized interview flows
- **Key tech:** JavaScript, Node.js backend, resume analysis + generative AI
- **Why it's interesting:** it's a practical example of using AI to personalize an experience rather than bolting a chatbot onto an existing product

### 📖 [Gen_AI — Generative AI Learning Journey](https://github.com/Ankitpatidar01/Gen_AI)

A running repository of hands-on Generative AI work — LangChain implementations, prompt engineering experiments, and RAG systems.

- **What it is:** the lab notebook behind everything above — where I first build and break things before they graduate into real projects like Tarka.AI
- **Key tech:** Python, LangChain, RAG, prompt engineering
